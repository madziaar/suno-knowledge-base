# MAG Music Records — Master Style Guide

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-01
**Foundation:** Rick Ross "Hood Billionaire" Luxury Trap

---

## Overview

This is the **canonical source of truth** for MAG Music Records' signature sound. All albums, regardless of language or regional variation, must follow this foundation unless explicitly overridden by a variation spec.

---

## Core Sound Identity

### Genre Classification
- **Primary:** Luxury Trap / Cinematic Hip-Hop
- **Secondary:** Boss Rap / Street Gospel
- **Influences:** Rick Ross, Maybach Music Group, Southern Trap

### BPM Range
| Type | BPM | Use Case |
|------|-----|----------|
| Slow/Reflective | 74-82 | Emotional tracks, closers |
| Standard | 83-90 | Most tracks, boss anthems |
| Energetic | 91-96 | Hype tracks, bangers |

**CRITICAL:** Never exceed 100 BPM. This is NOT drill, grime, or aggressive rap. The sound is SLOW, HEAVY, and DELIBERATE.

---

## Vocal Style

### Primary Voice Characteristics
| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Tone** | Deep, commanding baritone |
| **Texture** | Gravelly with natural rasp |
| **Projection** | Authoritative, confident |
| **Energy** | Relaxed but powerful |

### Flow & Cadence
- Measured, deliberate pacing
- Strategic pauses for emphasis
- Confident, boss-like rhythm
- NOT rushed or aggressive
- Calculated and composed

### Signature Ad-Libs
- "UGH!" / "Hunh!" (primary)
- "Yeah..." (reflective moments)
- "Boss!" / "MAG!" (brand callouts)
- Repetition of key phrases

### Vocal Production
- Layered vocals for depth
- Deep presence in the mix
- Slight reverb for space
- Clear, upfront delivery

## Production Elements

### Instrumentation (Required)
| Element | Description | Priority |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **808s** | Heavy, sustained, booming | ESSENTIAL |
| **Strings** | Orchestral, dramatic, cinematic | HIGH |
| **Brass** | Horns, trumpets, fanfares | HIGH |
| **Piano** | Grand piano elements | MEDIUM |
| **Pads** | Lush synth atmospheres | MEDIUM |
| **Choir** | Vocal samples, gospel influence | OPTIONAL |

### Drum Pattern Style
- Trap hi-hats (controlled, not excessive)
- Crisp snares with reverb tail
- Booming, punchy kicks
- Luxurious but street-ready percussion

### Mix Characteristics
- Vocals prominent and clear
- Deep, heavy low-end presence
- Wide, cinematic stereo field
- Polished but not over-produced
- Radio-ready quality

## Lyrical Themes

### Core Themes (Always Include)
1. **Success Narrative** — Rags to riches, came from nothing
2. **Boss Mentality** — Leadership, power, authority
3. **Luxury Lifestyle** — Cars, watches, champagne, success symbols
4. **Street Credibility** — Authentic roots, real experiences
5. **Loyalty** — Family, crew, blood ties
6. **Resilience** — Survival, overcoming obstacles

### Secondary Themes (Use as Needed)
- Romance/Relationships (street love angle)
- Spirituality/Faith (gratitude, blessings)
- Competition/Enemies (flames, opposition)
- Legacy/Impact (leaving a mark)
- Cultural Pride (heritage, roots)

### Lyrical Style
- Confident declarations, not questions
- Metaphors of power (kings, bosses, lions)
- Wealth imagery (gold, diamonds, luxury brands)
- Street wisdom and life lessons
- Motivational energy

## Song Structure

### Standard Track Structure
```
[Intro] — 8-16 bars, sets mood
[Verse 1] — 16 bars, establish narrative
[Hook/Chorus] — 8 bars, memorable, repeatable
[Verse 2] — 16 bars, develop story
[Hook/Chorus] — 8 bars
[Bridge] — 8 bars (optional), contrast
[Verse 3] — 16 bars (optional), conclusion
[Hook/Chorus] — 8 bars
[Outro] — 4-8 bars, fade or statement
```

### Track Length Targets
| Type | Length |
|------|--------|
| Intro | 1:30-2:30 |
| Standard | 3:30-4:30 |
| Extended/Closer | 4:30-6:00 |

## Quality Control Standards

### Before Approving Any Track:
- [ ] Vocal delivery sounds commanding and boss-like
- [ ] BPM is within specified range (74-96)
- [ ] Production has cinematic/orchestral elements
- [ ] 808s are heavy and present
- [ ] Overall vibe is luxury + street
- [ ] Lyrics match core themes
- [ ] Hook is memorable and repeatable
- [ ] Mix is clean and professional

### Red Flags (Reject If Present):
- High-pitched or nasal vocals
- BPM over 100 (too fast)
- Thin or weak bass
- Aggressive/screaming delivery
- Fast/choppy flow
- Missing orchestral elements
- Generic or off-brand content
