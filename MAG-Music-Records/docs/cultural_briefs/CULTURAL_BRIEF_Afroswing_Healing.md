## Part 1: Genre Definition - Afroswing

### What Is Afroswing?

Afroswing (also known as Afrobashment) is a UK-born genre that emerged in the mid-2010s, pioneered by artists like J Hus, Kojo Funds, Not3s, Yxng Bane, and MoStack. The name was coined by Kojo Funds.

**Kojo Funds' Definition:**
> "It's got the core of an African tune in the melody - that's where the 'afro' comes from - then you've got the mixture of drums from R&B and dancehall and that's what the 'swing' stands for."

### BPM Range

| Tempo | Usage |
|-------|-------|
| **95-115 BPM** | Typical Afroswing range |
| **100-108 BPM** | Sweet spot for most tracks |
| **105 BPM** | "Healing" approximate tempo |

**Note:** Afroswing is defined more by its melodic characteristics and drum patterns than strict tempo. The genre is flexible.

### Core Sound Elements

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Snare Placement** | On the THIRD beat (not fourth like hip-hop) - this is crucial |
| **Hi-hats** | Offbeat, dancehall-influenced patterns |
| **Bass** | Deep 808s from trap, but more melodic than aggressive |
| **Melody** | Happy or dark chords that "make you feel a certain way" |
| **Rhythm** | Polyrhythmic, syncopated, danceable |

### Production DNA

**Primary Influences:**
- Afrobeats (West African rhythms, polyrhythms)
- Dancehall (offbeat hi-hats, skanking rhythms)
- UK Grime (aggressive flows, street-edge intensity)
- Trap (808 bass, modern low-end foundation)
- R&B (smooth vocals, emotive phrasing)

**Key Production Elements:**
- Infectious guitar riffs
- Catchy melodic hooks
- Clean, polished mix
- Sparse percussion (avoid overcrowding)
- Shimmering synths
- Crisp snares

### How Afroswing Differs From Related Genres

| Genre | Key Difference |
|-------|----------------|
| **Afrobeats** | Afroswing adds UK grime/trap edge, MLE slang |
| **UK Drill** | Drill is darker, sliding 808s, more aggressive |
| **Grime** | Grime is faster (140 BPM), more aggressive delivery |
| **UK Rap** | Afroswing is more melodic, danceable, Afro-influenced |

## Part 2: "Healing" by Tion Wayne - Analysis

### Track Overview

- **Artist:** Tion Wayne (Dennis Junior Odunwo)
- **Released:** May 4, 2023
- **Producers:** Crumz & GA
- **Views:** 29M+ on YouTube
- **Style:** Blend of drill grit with Afroswing melody

### Thematic Framework

**Core Theme: Redemption and Growth**

Tion Wayne reflects on his journey from street life to success, acknowledging:
- The "madness" he went through
- Finding peace and tranquility
- Journey of healing and moving forward
- Transformation from struggle to prosperity

**Key Emotional Territory:**

| Theme | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| **Redemption** | Leaving behind past struggles |
| **Growth** | Personal evolution, maturity |
| **Reflection** | Looking back at the journey |
| **Triumph** | Making it despite the odds |
| **Gratitude** | Appreciating the journey |
| **Self-belief** | Knowing your worth all along |

### Why It Resonated

1. **Authenticity** - Tion Wayne's real experience (16-month prison stint, comeback)
2. **Relatability** - Many young people relate to overcoming adversity
3. **Musical Versatility** - Blends drill energy with melodic hooks
4. **Visual Storytelling** - Video shot in Nigeria, London, Ghana, India shows global ambition
5. **Emotional Honesty** - Raw reflection without being preachy

### Production Notes from "Healing"

- Gritty drill undertones with heavy 808 beat
- Melodic hooks soften the aggressive foundation
- Balances street credibility with accessible melody
- Energy builds throughout, triumphant feel

## Part 3: Authentic Language Bank

### Multicultural London English (MLE) - Core Vocabulary

MLE emerged from Caribbean (Jamaican Patois), West African, and British working-class influences. It is the authentic voice of Afroswing.

#### Greetings & Address

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Wagwan** | What's going on? | "Wagwan, you good?" |
| **Fam** | Close friend/family | "Trust me fam" |
| **Bruv** | Brother/close friend | "Listen bruv" |
| **Blud** | Close companion | "My blud" |
| **Mandem** | Your boys/crew (already plural) | "Me and the mandem" |
| **Gyaldem** | Group of girls | Refers to women collectively |

#### Positive Descriptors

| Term | Meaning | Usage |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Peng** | Attractive/high quality | "She's peng" / "That's peng" |
| **Lit** | Exciting/great | "This is lit" |
| **Safe** | All good/thank you | Multi-purpose positive |
| **Blessed** | Grateful/good | "I'm blessed, fam" |
| **Mad** | Crazy good | "That's mad" |

#### Intensifiers

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Bare** | A lot of/very | "Bare love for this" |
| **Proper** | Really/very | "Proper blessed" |
| **Well** | Very | "Well happy" |
| **Dead** | Extremely | "Dead serious" |

#### Common Expressions

| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **"You get me?"** | You understand? |
| **"Innit"** | Isn't it (confirmation) |
| **"Allow it"** | Let it go/forget it |
| **"Dun know"** | I understand/of course |
| **"Trust"** | Believe me |
| **"No cap"** | No lie |
| **"On my life"** | I swear |
| **"Real talk"** | Being honest |

#### Movement/Life Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Ends** | Your neighborhood |
| **Road** | Street life |
| **Leveling up** | Improving/growing |
| **Graft** | Hard work/hustle |
| **Pattern** | To sort out/arrange |
| **Link** | Meet up |

#### Success/Money Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **P's** | Money (pounds) |
| **Bands** | Money |
| **Balling** | Living well |
| **Drip** | Style/fashion |
| **Fresh** | Clean/well-dressed |

### Phrases to Use Naturally

**For Healing/Growth Theme:**
- "Been through the madness, now I'm blessed"
- "From the ends to the top, you get me"
- "Proper graft got me here"
- "Leveling up, no looking back"
- "Bare love for the journey"
- "Trust, the pain made me stronger"

**For Triumph/Success:**
- "They doubted, now they see me"
- "My mandem always knew"
- "Safe in my zone now"
- "Dun know I was destined"
- "Real ones stayed, innit"

### Phrases That Feel FORCED - Avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| "Cheerio mate" | Too stereotypical British |
| "Blimey" | Wrong register, not MLE |
| "Bloody hell" | More general British, not urban |
| "Guv'nor" | Cockney, not MLE |
| Overusing "innit" every line | Sounds like parody |
| "Roadman ting" | Self-aware/mocking |

## Part 5: Production Notes for Suno

### Afroswing Prompt Template

```
UK Afroswing, [BPM] BPM, Snare on Third Beat, Offbeat Hi-hats, Melodic 808 Bass, [Chord Mood] Chords, Infectious Guitar Riff, Smooth Male Vocals, [Emotion], MLE Delivery, Clean Polished Mix
```

### Example Prompts

**For "Healing" Vibe:**
```
UK Afroswing, 105 BPM, Snare on Third Beat, Offbeat Hi-hats, Deep Melodic 808, Warm Major Chords, Shimmering Synths, Smooth Male Vocals with Light Auto-tune, Reflective Triumphant Energy, British Urban Delivery, Danceable Groove, Professional Mix
```

**For More Upbeat Track:**
```
UK Afroswing, 108 BPM, Dancehall-influenced Drums, Afrobeats Polyrhythm, Catchy Guitar Hook, Sparse Percussion, Happy Chords, Energetic Male Vocals, Celebratory Mood, London Street Vibe, Clean Production
```

**For Darker Reflective Track:**
```
UK Afroswing, 100 BPM, Moody Chords, Deep 808 Bass, Crisp Snares on Third, Atmospheric Pads, Smooth Introspective Male Vocals, Street-Edge Intensity, Redemption Theme, Polished Mix
```

### Key Production Terms for Suno

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| "Snare on third beat" | "Like J Hus" |
| "Afrobeats-influenced rhythm" | Artist names |
| "MLE delivery" | "Roadman style" |
| "UK Afroswing" | "Afrobashment" (less recognized) |
| "Dancehall hi-hats" | "Skank" (can trigger filters) |
| "Offbeat rhythm guitar" | "Reggae skank guitar" |
| "Clean polished mix" | "Professional studio quality" |

### What Makes Afroswing Work in Suno

1. **Melodic Focus** - Strong hooks and catchy melodies
2. **Rhythmic Identity** - That third-beat snare is essential
3. **Sparse Arrangement** - Don't overcrowd the mix
4. **Vocal Space** - Let the vocals breathe
5. **Groove First** - If it's not danceable, it's not Afroswing
