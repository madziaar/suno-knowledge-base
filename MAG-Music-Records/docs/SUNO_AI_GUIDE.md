# Suno AI Music Generation Guide 2025

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-01
**Purpose:** Comprehensive guide for using Suno AI for music production at MAG Music Records

---

## Overview

Suno AI is an AI-powered music creation tool that generates original songs, lyrics, and instrumentals. It can create full songs in under 30 seconds with just a topic/lyrics and style/genre input.

---

## Two Main Modes

### 1. Simple Mode
- Create music using just a prompt
- Think of a theme, mood, or story
- Example: "a relaxing beach melody" or "a high-energy rock anthem"
- Suno generates two versions of your song with a single click

### 2. Custom Mode (Recommended for MAG Productions)
- More granular control over songwriting
- Separate lyric generation from musical style prompts
- Fine-tune lyrics and music independently
- Results in more personalized outputs

## Components of Effective Prompts

### Genre/Style
The musical category sets the overall feel. Examples:
- Pop, Rock, Jazz, Electronic
- Luxury Trap, Cinematic Hip-Hop
- Afro-Spiritual, Gospel Fusion

### Mood/Emotion
Words that guide the AI in setting the right tone:
- Happy, Melancholic, Energetic
- Powerful, Commanding, Reflective
- Spiritual, Meditative, Aggressive

### Instrumentation
Specify key instruments to shape the sonic palette:
- Piano, Electric guitar, Orchestral strings
- Heavy 808 bass, Brass stabs, Choir samples
- Oud, Ney flute, Djembe

### Tempo/BPM
Always include BPM range:
- 70-85 BPM (slow, deliberate - Rick Ross style)
- 85-95 BPM (moderate energy)
- 95-120 BPM (high energy)

### Vocal Style
Specify vocal characteristics:
- Deep male vocals, Commanding delivery
- Female ethereal vocals, Whispered
- Male and female duet

## Best Practices for Prompts

### DO: Be Specific
Instead of "happy song," write:
"A lively pop song with upbeat rhythms and cheerful lyrics about summer adventures"

### DO: Write Descriptions, Not Commands
- BAD: "Create an upbeat pop track..."
- GOOD: "Upbeat pop track..."

### DO: Combine Elements Creatively
Instead of "adventure rock", combine:
- Genres + Instruments + Atmosphere + Dynamics + Rhythm + Emotions

### DO: Use Era/Decade References
"1980s influence, Synthwave, Nostalgic, Synthesizers, 80s futurism, Analog warmth"

### DON'T: Use Artist Names
Due to copyright restrictions, avoid artist/band names. Instead, describe characteristics:
- BAD: "Like Rick Ross"
- GOOD: "Deep commanding male vocals, deliberate slow delivery, boss rap style"

## Metatags for Lyrics

Use square brackets to structure your song:

### Structure Tags
- `[Intro]` - Opening section
- `[Verse 1]`, `[Verse 2]` - Verse sections
- `[Pre-Chorus]` - Build-up to chorus
- `[Chorus]` or `[Hook]` - Main hook
- `[Bridge]` - Contrasting section
- `[Outro]` - Closing section
- `[Break]` - Instrumental break

### Voice/Style Tags
- `[Male Vocal]`, `[Female Vocal]`
- `[Spoken Word]`, `[Whispered]`
- `[Ad-lib]`, `[Harmonies]`

### Sound Effect Tags
- `(Instrumental)`, `(Orchestra swells)`
- `(Bass drops)`, `(Drums fade)`

### Advanced Tags (V4.5+)
- Multi-layered blocks: `[Verse 2][Add tension → remove drums → expose vocals]`
- Core ingredients: `[synth pads, electronic drums, deep bass]`

## Tips for Better Results

### 1. Use the Enhancement Feature (V4.5+)
After typing your prompt, tap the sparkle icon at the top-right to enhance with extra creative elements.

### 2. Design Emotional Progression
Structure your prompt around emotional arcs:
- Tension to relief
- Sadness to hope
- Peace to chaos

### 3. Iterate and Refine
- First version might not be perfect
- Experiment with different prompts
- Adjust lyrics and fine-tune results

### 4. Match Prompt Length to Complexity
- Simple songs: Short, focused prompts
- Complex productions: Detailed, layered prompts

## Lyrics Formatting Best Practices

### Structure
```
[Intro]
(Instrumental description or ad-libs)

[Verse 1]
Line 1
Line 2
Line 3
Line 4

[Pre-Chorus]
Build-up lines

[Chorus]
Hook lines (most memorable)
Repeat key phrase

[Verse 2]
Continue narrative

[Bridge]
Contrasting section

[Outro]
Closing lines or fade
```

### Portuguese/Non-English Lyrics
- Use ASCII-friendly characters when possible
- Avoid special characters that might cause issues
- Keep section markers in English (Suno recognizes them better)
