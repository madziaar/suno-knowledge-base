# Original Music Creation — Compliance, Safety & Creative Standards

**CRITICAL DOCUMENT — READ BEFORE CREATING ANY CONTENT**

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

> **We may copy the FEELING. We may NOT copy the FINGERPRINT.**

"Fingerprint" includes anything that automated systems or human reviewers could identify as coming from an existing song, artist, or album.

**If a machine could match it, it is not allowed.**

---

## SECTION 1 — STRICTLY FORBIDDEN (NO-GO ZONES)

The following are **ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED**:

### Lyrics
1. Copying lyrics from any existing song
2. Paraphrasing lyrics from any existing song
3. Translating lyrics from any existing song into another language
4. Reusing or closely mimicking hooks, choruses, intros, or outros

### Melodies & Music
5. Recreating melodies from any existing song
6. Matching chord progressions AND tempo AND structure simultaneously
7. Remaking beats, instrumentals, or arrangements from known songs
8. Sampling audio of any kind without explicit clearance

### Delivery & Style
9. Reproducing ad-lib patterns unique to a specific track
10. Matching BPM + cadence + delivery + silence timing together
11. Referencing existing lyrics internally while writing new lyrics

### Structure
12. Structuring songs track-by-track as direct musical equivalents of another album

**If any of the above occurs: STOP AND REDESIGN.**

---

## SECTION 2 — WHAT IS ALLOWED (SAFE INSPIRATION)

### Style & Atmosphere (ALLOWED)
- General genre characteristics (luxury trap, cinematic hip-hop)
- Mood and energy levels (boss energy, reflective, triumphant)
- Production aesthetics (orchestral, heavy bass, cinematic)
- Vocal tone descriptions (deep, commanding, authoritative)
- BPM ranges (as long as not matched with other elements)

### Original Content (REQUIRED)
- 100% original lyrics
- Original hooks and choruses
- Original melodies
- Original song structures
- Original ad-lib patterns
- Original narratives and stories

### Reference Usage (ALLOWED)
- Referencing an artist's general delivery style
- Referencing a genre's typical production elements
- Referencing a mood or atmosphere
- Referencing tempo ranges typical of a genre

---

## SECTION 3 — HOW TO SAFELY REFERENCE

### WRONG (FORBIDDEN):
```
"Make it sound like Rick Ross's 'The Devil Is a Lie'"
"Copy the hook style from 'Sanctified'"
"Use the same flow as [specific song]"
```

### RIGHT (ALLOWED):
```
"Deep, commanding male vocals with measured delivery"
"Luxury trap with orchestral elements and heavy 808s"
"Boss energy, triumphant mood, cinematic production"
"BPM range 85-90, deliberate pacing"
```

---

## SECTION 4 — COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

Before finalizing any track, verify:

### Lyrics Check
- [ ] All lyrics are 100% original
- [ ] No paraphrasing of existing songs
- [ ] No translated lyrics from other songs
- [ ] Hook is completely original
- [ ] No recognizable phrases from other tracks

### Music Check
- [ ] Melody is original
- [ ] Beat/instrumental is original
- [ ] Not matching multiple elements from a single song
- [ ] No unauthorized samples

### Delivery Check
- [ ] Ad-lib patterns are original
- [ ] Flow pattern is original
- [ ] Not mimicking a specific song's delivery

### Structure Check
- [ ] Song structure is original
- [ ] Not track-for-track copying an album
- [ ] Arrangement is unique

---

## SECTION 5 — WHAT TO DO IF UNCERTAIN

If you're unsure whether something crosses the line:

1. **STOP** - Do not proceed
2. **ASK** - Verify with user before continuing
3. **REDESIGN** - Create something new that doesn't raise concerns
4. **DOCUMENT** - Note the decision for future reference

---

## SECTION 6 — WHY THIS MATTERS

Copyright infringement can result in:
- Content takedowns
- Financial claims and lawsuits
- Platform bans
- Reputation damage
- Lost revenue

**Prevention is easier than remediation.**
