# MAG Music Records — Creative Philosophy

*A compass, not a manual.*

---

## The Only Rule

**Make music you enjoy making.**

Everything else is optional.

---

## What Success Looks Like

Success is not:
- A release
- A stream count
- External approval

Success is:
- Showing up
- Creating something
- Enjoying the process
- Learning one small thing
- Finishing what you started (when you feel like it)

---

## The Three Principles

### 1. Simple

Do one thing at a time.
Start with the hook.
If it's not working, move on.
Templates exist so you don't have to think about structure.
Let the system handle the boring parts.

### 2. Easy

Low friction, high flow.
No pressure to be perfect.
First drafts are supposed to be rough.
Copy, paste, done.
If it takes more than 5 minutes to set up, something is wrong.

### 3. Fun

If it stops being fun, stop.
Come back tomorrow.
There's no deadline that matters more than your energy.
Play with sounds. Experiment. Break rules.
The worst that happens is you try again.

---

## Permission Slips

You are allowed to:
- Make something terrible
- Not finish
- Change your mind
- Take a break
- Not release it
- Release it anyway
- Make the same type of song over and over
- Make something completely different
- Work for 10 minutes or 10 hours
- Feel uninspired and still create

---

## What to Do When Stuck

1. **Step away.** Not every day is a creative day.
2. **Start smaller.** Just the hook. Just one line.
3. **Use a template.** Don't build from scratch.
4. **Change the input.** Listen to something different.
5. **Lower the stakes.** This one doesn't have to be good.

---

## Consistency Over Intensity

A little bit often beats a lot once in a while.

5 minutes today > 5 hours "someday"

The habit matters more than the output.

---

## Growth Happens Quietly

You won't notice you're getting better.
Then one day you will.

Trust the process.
The reps count even when they don't feel like it.

---

## Release When Ready

Releasing is optional.
But if you want to:

- It doesn't have to be perfect
- It just has to be done
- The next one will be better
- The one after that, even better

Each release is practice, not performance.

---

## When in Doubt

Ask yourself:
- *Am I enjoying this?*
- *What's the smallest next step?*
- *Does this need to be harder than it is?*

If the answer is no, adjust.

---

## The Reset

Whenever you feel pressure, obligation, or resistance:

Come back here.
Read this page.
Remember why you started.

Then make something.
Or don't.
Both are fine.

---

*This is your project. Your pace. Your rules.*
