## Production Notes

### Suno Tips
- Keep prompts under 2000 chars for best results
- Specify exact BPM (140 for drill)
- Include "UK Drill" and "aggressive 808s" tags
- Use "[Verse]" and "[Chorus]" markers in lyrics

### Sound Design
- Layer 808s with subtle distortion
- Hi-hats at 1/16 with occasional rolls
- Dark pads for atmosphere
- Vocal processing: slight autotune, reverb tail
