# Cultural Brief: MAG Bounce Vol. 1

**Created:** 2026-01-04
**Reference:** "Ignition (Remix)" vibe — carefree party bounce R&B
**Setting:** UK yacht party / Upper Class celebration

---

## Cultural Context

### The Vibe We're Capturing
The carefree, feel-good party energy of early 2000s bounce R&B — playful, confident, celebratory. Not aggressive or boastful, but joyful and inclusive. The kind of energy where everyone's having a good time and the music makes you move.

**Key emotions:**
- Carefree joy
- Confident but not arrogant
- Celebratory / grateful
- Flirtatious but tasteful
- Living in the moment

### The Setting: UK Yacht Party
Mediterranean waters, golden hour, well-dressed guests, champagne flowing. This isn't a club — it's an elevated, exclusive gathering of people who've earned their success. Think Monaco harbour, Ibiza sunset, French Riviera.

## What to AVOID

### Stereotypes & Inauthenticity
- ❌ Over-the-top "posh" caricatures ("pip pip cheerio")
- ❌ Forced nautical metaphors
- ❌ American party slang that doesn't fit UK context
- ❌ Aggressive boasting about wealth
- ❌ Explicit content (keep it tasteful)
- ❌ Violence/weapons references

### Specific Terms to Avoid
- ❌ "Innit" (too casual/street for yacht setting)
- ❌ "Bruv/Fam" (wrong register for Upper Class)
- ❌ "Lit" (American slang)
- ❌ "Turn up" (American slang)
- ❌ Heavy flex language

## Song Structure Guidance

### Hooks Should Feel:
- Singable and memorable
- Celebratory but not aggressive
- Easy to chant along to
- Positive and uplifting

### Verses Should:
- Paint the scene (yacht, sunset, people)
- Express gratitude for the journey
- Build connection with listeners
- Feel conversational, not preachy

### Bridge Should:
- Provide emotional depth
- Moment of reflection
- Then return to celebration

## Reference Mood Board

**Visual:** Golden hour on water, white yacht, well-dressed guests in linen/summer attire, champagne glasses, Mediterranean coastline, string lights at dusk

**Sound:** Mid-tempo groove (92-100 BPM), Rhodes chords, groovy bassline, light percussion, tropical accents, smooth vocals

**Energy:** A Sunday afternoon that turns into a memorable evening
