# PROJECT: MAG Hood Boss UK Vol. 1

## OVERVIEW

I need you to work on a 12-track UK English luxury trap album called "MAG Hood Boss UK Vol. 1". This is the UK version of our Portuguese "MAG Hood Boss Vol. 1" album.

## CRITICAL SONIC DIRECTION

**THIS IS NOT UK DRILL.** This is Rick Ross "Hood Billionaire" style luxury trap with UK English slang.

### MUST MAINTAIN:
- **BPM:** 80-95 BPM (slow, deliberate, boss tempo)
- **808s:** Heavy, sustained, booming (NOT sliding/gliding drill 808s)
- **Production:** Orchestral strings, brass fanfares, piano, lush atmospheric pads
- **Vocals:** Deep, commanding, authoritative male voice
- **Energy:** Luxury, wealth, boss arrival, street credibility
- **Ad-libs:** "UGH!", "YEAH", "MAG", "You know"

### MUST AVOID:
- UK Drill sound (140+ BPM, sliding 808s, aggressive hi-hats)
- Grime production style
- Fast, choppy flows
- American slang (no "gang gang", "lit", "finna")

## REFERENCE ARTISTS

Think UK artists doing SLOW luxury trap:
- Giggs "Landlord" era
- Skepta's slower boss tracks
- Headie One's melodic tracks
- Krept & Konan luxury sound

The sound should feel like Rick Ross performing in London - same tempo, same weight, same boss energy, just with UK vocabulary.
