## Instructions for Use

### When to Log a Decision

Record decisions that:
- Change the creative direction of a track or project
- Affect release timelines or distribution
- Involve credits, splits, or legal matters
- Resolve disputes or disagreements
- Deviate from the original plan
- Have budget implications

### How to Log

1. **ID:** Sequential number (001, 002, etc.)
2. **Date:** When the decision was made (YYYY-MM-DD)
3. **Track/Area:** Which track or project area is affected
4. **Decision:** Clear, concise statement of what was decided
5. **Rationale:** Brief explanation of why this decision was made
6. **Approved By:** Name of person(s) who authorized the decision

### Best Practices

- Log decisions as soon as they are made
- Be specific and factual
- Avoid vague language
- Reference related documents where applicable
- Review the log during project retrospectives
