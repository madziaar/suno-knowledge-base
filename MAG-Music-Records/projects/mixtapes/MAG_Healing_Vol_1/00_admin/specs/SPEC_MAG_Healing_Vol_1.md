## Sound Identity

### Core Elements
- **Drums:** Snare on THIRD beat (defining characteristic)
- **Hi-hats:** Offbeat, dancehall-influenced
- **Bass:** Deep melodic 808s (not aggressive drill 808s)
- **Melody:** Major/happy chords with emotional depth
- **Vocals:** Smooth male vocals, light auto-tune, MLE delivery

### Production Palette
- Infectious guitar riffs
- Shimmering synths
- Afrobeats polyrhythms
- Sparse, clean arrangement
- Professional polished mix
