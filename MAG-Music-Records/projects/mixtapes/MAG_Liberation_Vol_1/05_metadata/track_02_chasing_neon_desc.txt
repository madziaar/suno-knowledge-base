Chasing Neon captures the intoxicating freedom of losing yourself in the city at night. Inspired by the vibrant UK garage and dance-pop sound of the late 90s, this track pulses with the energy of urban nightlife where anonymity becomes liberation. The production sparkles with shimmering synths and driving rhythms that mirror the glow of streetlights and club marquees. The lyrics celebrate the joy of moving through crowds of strangers, the music pulling you forward, no destination needed. This is the soundtrack to that electric feeling when the night opens up and anything feels possible. Lost in colour, found in sound. A love letter to the dancefloors, the back streets, and the neon-lit moments that make us feel truly alive.
