When Morning Comes closes MAG Liberation Vol. 1 with the quiet clarity that follows a long night's journey toward dawn. In the tradition of the emotional album closers that defined late-90s UK dance music, this track offers peaceful resolution after the euphoria. The production breathes with gentle warmth, spacious pads and soft rhythms creating the sonic equivalent of first light breaking through. The lyrics speak of chains that no longer remain, of seeing clearly after the night has passed, of arriving somewhere new while standing in the same place. This is liberation complete. Not the explosive release of earlier tracks, but the settled peace of transformation integrated. Breathe in the light. Let go the night. Everything is right.
