Used To Be Us is a tender reflection on love that has run its course, delivered without bitterness or regret. In the tradition of the emotional ballads that balanced late-90s UK dance albums, this track finds beauty in acceptance. The production wraps gentle piano and warm atmospheric textures around a narrative of gratitude for what was, rather than grief for what ended. The lyrics honour a past relationship with grace, acknowledging that some things are meant to end, and that ending can be beautiful too. Rivers diverge. People grow. And sometimes the most loving thing we can do is wish someone well from afar. A mature, emotionally intelligent meditation on letting go while holding onto the good.
