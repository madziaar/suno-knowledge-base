# MAG Liberation Vol. 1 - Tracklist

**Artist:** MAG Music Records
**Style:** Late-90s UK Dance/Pop
**Vocals:** Female, soft, confident, breathy but controlled
**BPM Range:** 125-130 BPM
**Core Vibe:** Liberation, relief, light euphoria, emotional release
**Total Tracks:** 8 (7 English, 1 Portuguese)

---

## Album Concept

A journey through the emotional landscape of letting go and finding freedom. The album captures that specific late-90s UK dance feeling - hands in the air, eyes closed, lost in the moment. Each track represents a stage of liberation: from the first spark of wanting more, through the messy process of releasing what holds us back, to the clarity that comes with dawn.

## Production Notes

- **Vocal Character:** Soft, breathy, confident without being aggressive. Think late-90s UK dance divas - emotional authenticity over technical showmanship.
- **Instrumentation:** Piano hooks, euphoric synth pads, four-on-the-floor kicks, shimmering hi-hats, occasional orchestral swells for emotional peaks.
- **Mix Aesthetic:** Warm, spacious, radio-friendly but club-ready. Vocals sit prominently but never fight the groove.
- **Avoid:** Aggressive drops, dark atmospheres, overly processed vocals, anything that breaks the nostalgic warmth.
