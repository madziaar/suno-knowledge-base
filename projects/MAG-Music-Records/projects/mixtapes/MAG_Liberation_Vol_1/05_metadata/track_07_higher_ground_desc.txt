Higher Ground delivers a triumphant anthem of personal resurrection. Channelling the uplifting spirit of late-90s UK dance music at its most empowering, this track transforms struggle into strength. The production builds from grounded determination to soaring release, with powerful beats and euphoric synths lifting the listener higher with each progression. The lyrics speak to everyone who has been down so long they forgot what the sky looks like, then discovered something shifting inside, something waking up. This is the moment of rising, of leaving shadows on the ground, of finding where you belong. The spirit ignites. The heart reaches for stars. Nothing can bring you down when you have finally found your higher ground.
