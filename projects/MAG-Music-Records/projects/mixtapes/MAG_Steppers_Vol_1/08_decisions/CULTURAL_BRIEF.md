## Concept: UK Stepping Soul

Blending **Chicago Stepping** culture with **British R&B/Lovers Rock** sensibility.

### Foundation Elements
| Chicago Stepping | UK Influence |
|------------------|--------------|
| 8-count pattern | UK Garage smoothness |
| Partner dancing | Lovers Rock romance |
| "Give me game" | British slang |
| Elegant, classy | Well-dressed, sophisticated |
| Side to side motion | Craig David-era R&B |

## Style Notes

### DO Include
- British slang naturally woven in
- Sophisticated, grown & sexy UK vibe
- Craig David-style smoothness
- Lovers Rock romantic sensibility
- Partner dance references
- Classy, well-dressed imagery

### DON'T Include
- American slang (shorty, boo, etc.)
- Over-the-top UK stereotypes
- Cockney rhyming slang (too comedic)
- Artist name references in prompts
- Copying any existing lyrics

---

## Authenticity Checklist

Before finalizing lyrics:
- [ ] British slang sounds natural, not forced
- [ ] Stepping references are accurate
- [ ] Romance is sophisticated, not crude
- [ ] Would fit on a Craig David or Lovers Rock playlist
- [ ] No copyright issues
- [ ] Flows smoothly when sung
