Healing is a reflective UK Afroswing anthem about finding peace after life's struggles. Built on warm synth pads and a melodic 808 bassline at 105 BPM, the track captures the journey from darkness to light. Smooth male vocals deliver an honest narrative of personal growth, self-discovery, and gratitude. The offbeat hi-hats and snare groove create a laid-back yet uplifting atmosphere that moves both body and soul. This is music for late nights of reflection and early mornings of new beginnings. A testament to resilience and the power of inner transformation.
