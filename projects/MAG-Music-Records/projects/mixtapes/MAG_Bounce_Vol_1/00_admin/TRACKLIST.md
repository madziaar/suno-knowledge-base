## Track Themes

### Track 1: Deck Party
Opening track. Setting the scene — arriving at the yacht, the party's starting, anticipation and excitement. High energy introduction.

### Track 2: Sunset Groove
Golden hour vibes. Romantic undertones, watching the sunset with someone special. Medium tempo, smooth and warm.

### Track 3: Champagne Waves
Peak carefree energy. Living in the moment, champagne flowing, dancing on deck. Celebratory and fun.

### Track 4: Deep Blue
Deeper romantic connection. Locked eyes across the deck, intimate moment amid the party. Lower energy, emotional.

### Track 5: All Night
Party at its peak. Everyone dancing, energy maxed out, the night is young. Highest energy track.

### Track 6: Last Dance
Closing the night. Memorable moments, gratitude for the experience, promise of more to come. Reflective but warm.
