## Album Message

> **"O amor conquista tudo. Juntos, somos imparáveis."**

Every song is part of ONE love story:
- Track 1: Meeting/First Impression
- Track 2: Facing opposition together
- Track 3: Celebrating the relationship
- Track 4: Deepening intimacy
- Track 5: Reflecting on the journey
- Track 6: Committing to the future

## Production Notes

- All tracks: Island percussion, reggae influences
- Vocals: Melodic singing, smooth delivery, tasteful Auto-Tune
- Language: European Portuguese (Portugal) upper class throughout
- NO: Street slang, trap beats, aggressive delivery
- YES: Warmth, romance, sophistication, positivity

### Portuguese-Specific Notes
- Use European Portuguese pronunciation cues in prompts
- "Tu" form for intimate address
- Avoid Brazilian Portuguese expressions
- Refined aristocratic vocabulary
