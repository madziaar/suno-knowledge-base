# MAG Refined PT Vol. 1 — Style Reference

## Sound Identity

**Foundation:** Akon "Konvicted" era (2006-2008) — Reggae Fusion R&B

**NOT Rick Ross Luxury Trap.** This is smooth, melodic, island-influenced R&B.

**Language:** European Portuguese (Portugal) — Upper Class/Aristocratic register

---

## Core Sound DNA

### Reference Tracks
| Track | Artist | Use For |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Don't Matter | Akon | Overall vibe, reggae groove, optimism |
| Smack That | Akon ft. Eminem | Energy, club appeal |
| I Wanna Love You | Akon ft. Snoop Dogg | Romantic feel, smooth delivery |
| Beautiful | Akon | Melodic structure, emotional depth |

### Production Elements
| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **BPM** | 95-110 (dancehall/reggae groove) |
| **Drums** | Island percussion, reggae one-drop, snare on 3 |
| **Bass** | Warm, melodic bass lines (not 808 trap) |
| **Keys** | Synth pads, Rhodes, warm chords |
| **Guitar** | Reggae skank, clean electric, acoustic strums |
| **Percussion** | Shakers, bongos, congas, island textures |
| **Vocals** | Melodic singing, smooth, Auto-Tune warmth |

### What This Is NOT
- NOT trap or drill
- NOT heavy 808s
- NOT aggressive delivery
- NOT street slang
- NOT fast rap flows

---

## Vocal Style

### Delivery
- **Melodic singing** — Not rap, not spoken word
- **Smooth, warm tone** — Think Akon's distinctive voice
- **Auto-Tune as instrument** — Subtle, warm, not robotic
- **Layered harmonies** — Rich vocal arrangements
- **Falsetto moments** — Emotional peaks

### Portuguese Upper Class Language

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| "Querida", "Minha cara" | "Gata", "Mana" |
| "Esplêndido", "Magnífico" | "Fixe", "Massa" |
| "Parece-me que...", "Devo dizer" | "Tipo...", "Tá-se bem" |
| "Absolutamente extraordinário" | "Bué fixe" |
| "Irei", "Hei de" | "Vou", "Tou a ir" |
| Proper European Portuguese | Brazilian expressions |

### Refined Vocabulary Examples
```
Street: "Ela é bué boa, tá-se bem"
Refined: "Ela é requintada, elegância personificada"

Street: "Tamos a fazer guito, sem cap"
Refined: "A fortuna sorri-nos, sem qualquer dúvida"

Street: "Vamos ao spot, vai ser fixe"
Refined: "Faremos a nossa chegada ao estabelecimento"
```

## Thematic Guidelines

### Track Theme Distribution (6-track EP)
1. **Opening Statement** — Sophisticated introduction, sets the refined tone
2. **Romantic** — Love, devotion, like "Don't Matter"
3. **Feel-Good/Optimistic** — Positivity, celebration
4. **Sensual/Intimate** — Tasteful romance, not explicit
5. **Reflective** — Gratitude, perspective, wisdom
6. **Anthemic Closer** — Uplifting, memorable finale

### Lyrical Approach
- **Romantic without crudeness** — Tasteful expressions of love
- **Optimistic without naive** — Earned positivity
- **Sophisticated without pretentious** — Natural refinement
- **Emotional without melodramatic** — Genuine feeling

## Core Message: "Sem Dúvida" (Don't Matter)

### Us Against The World
> **"Não querem ver-nos juntos, mas isso não importa"**

- External critics, naysayers, doubters
- Love persists despite opposition
- Partner reassurance: "Estou contigo, independentemente de tudo"
- Defiant but not aggressive — confident and calm

### Unconditional Devotion
- Love without conditions or requirements
- Standing by your partner through anything
- "No final do dia, tenho as tuas costas"
- Loyalty as the highest value

### Reassuring Your Partner
- Direct address: speaking TO her, not about her
- Calming her fears and doubts
- "Não te preocupes com o que dizem"
- Protective but not possessive

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any track:

- [ ] BPM is 95-110 (not trap tempo)
- [ ] Island/reggae percussion present
- [ ] Melodic singing delivery (not rap)
- [ ] European Portuguese vocabulary used
- [ ] No street slang (no "bué", "fixe", "mano")
- [ ] Romantic/positive themes
- [ ] Warm, smooth production feel
- [ ] Auto-Tune used tastefully (not excessively)
- [ ] Direct address to partner (tu/te)
- [ ] "Us vs them" element where appropriate
- [ ] 100% original content (COMPLIANCE check)
