# Authorial Context — MAG Refined PT Vol. 1

**CRITICAL:** Apply this context to ALL lyrics in this project.

---

## Authorial Perspective

- **I am a grown man writing in the first person**
- The voice is masculine, composed, confident, emotionally controlled
- This is NOT storytelling about "a man" — I AM the speaker
- NO boyish tone, NO immaturity, NO desperation, NO pleading

---

## Relational Context

- The opposite sex referenced is female
- Expressions of attraction, desire, love must be:
  - Controlled
  - Respectful
  - Self-possessed
  - Non-needy
- Power dynamics: calm and assured, NOT reactive or performative

---

## Language Distribution

| Language | Usage | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| **European Portuguese** | Primary (~90%) | Upper-class, educated, elegant |
| **English** | Secondary (~5%) | Intentional, limited |
| **Guinea-Bissau Creole** | Tertiary (~5% MAX) | Emotional emphasis, cultural authenticity, short phrases only |

### Creole Guidelines
- Used sparingly for emotional emphasis
- Cultural authenticity moments
- Short phrases or single lines ONLY
- NEVER dominate verses or hooks

---

## Style & Register

### Portuguese Must Sound:
- Educated
- Elegant
- Mature
- International

### Avoid:
- Slang-heavy phrasing
- Brazilian Portuguese structures/vocabulary
- Overly colloquial or street language

---

## Intent

- Lyrics should feel **intentional, premium, adult**
- Emotional expression suggests **experience and restraint**, not chaos
- Every line should sound like it comes from **someone who knows who he is**

---

## Quality Check Questions

Before finalizing any lyrics, ask:
1. Does this sound like a grown man speaking?
2. Is the emotional expression controlled or chaotic?
3. Is there any neediness or desperation?
4. Is the Creole usage under 5% and purposeful?
5. Does every line sound self-assured?
