# Akon Message Reference — Album-Wide Messaging

## ALBUM COHESION: "Konvicted" Era

This ENTIRE EP must feel like one cohesive Akon-style project. Every track shares:

### The Unified Album Message
> **"Love conquers all. Together, we're unstoppable."**

Every song on this EP is part of ONE love story:
- Track 1: Meeting/First Impression
- Track 2: Facing opposition together
- Track 3: Celebrating the relationship
- Track 4: Deepening intimacy
- Track 5: Reflecting on the journey
- Track 6: Committing to the future

### Recurring Elements Across ALL Tracks
1. **"Us vs. Them"** — External doubters, but love wins
2. **Direct Address** — Always speaking TO her
3. **Reassurance** — "I've got you" energy
4. **Optimism** — Problems mentioned, but always resolved
5. **Refined Devotion** — Upper class gentleman protecting his lady

## Core Message: "Don't Matter"

The defining message of Akon's romantic tracks:

### Us Against The World
> **"Nobody wanna see us together, but it don't matter"**

- External critics, naysayers, doubters
- Love persists despite opposition
- Partner reassurance: "I've got you regardless"
- Defiant but not aggressive — confident and calm

### Unconditional Devotion
- Love without conditions or requirements
- Standing by your partner through anything
- "Cause at the end of the day I got your back"
- Loyalty as the highest value

### Reassuring Your Partner
- Direct address: speaking TO her, not about her
- Calming her fears and doubts
- "Don't worry about what they say"
- Protective but not possessive

### Optimistic Defiance
- Choosing love over fear
- Future-focused: things will work out
- Not dwelling on problems, focusing on solutions
- "We're gonna make it through"

## Lyrical Patterns to Emulate

### Structure
1. **Verse:** Acknowledge the problem/obstacle
2. **Pre-Chorus:** Dismiss the negativity
3. **Chorus:** Affirm the commitment/love
4. **Bridge:** Deeper declaration

### Emotional Arc
```
Problem → Dismissal → Affirmation → Resolution
"They say..." → "But it don't matter..." → "I love you..." → "We'll be fine..."
```

### Direct Address (Second Person)
Write TO the partner:
```
"You're the only one I see"
"When you need me, I'll be there"
"Don't let them get to you, darling"
```

NOT about her in third person:
```
AVOID: "She's so beautiful, she's my queen"
USE: "You're so beautiful, you're my queen"
```

## Forbidden in Lyrics

- Street slang ("shawty", "shorty", "bae")
- Crude or explicit content
- Aggressive or confrontational tone
- Materialism without purpose
- Generic love song clichés without the Akon "defiant devotion" angle

## Required in Lyrics

- Direct address to partner (you/your)
- Us-against-the-world element in at least 4 of 6 tracks
- Reassurance and protection themes
- Optimistic resolution in each song
- Refined UK vocabulary throughout
