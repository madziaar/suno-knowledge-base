# Variation Spec: UK English

**Variation ID:** UK_ENGLISH
**Base:** MASTER_STYLE_GUIDE.md
**Language:** UK English with Roadman/MLE Slang

---

## Overview

This variation adapts the MAG luxury trap sound for UK English audiences. The SOUND remains the same (Rick Ross style luxury trap), but the LANGUAGE uses authentic UK roadman slang and London/UK cultural references.

**CRITICAL:** This is NOT UK Drill. Keep the slow, heavy, luxury trap sound (80-95 BPM). Only the language changes.

---

| Element | Master (Portuguese) | UK English Adaptation |

## What Stays the Same

- BPM range: 74-96 (NOT 140+ drill tempo)
- Vocal style: Deep, commanding, boss-like
- Production: Orchestral, cinematic, heavy 808s
- Themes: Success, boss mentality, loyalty, luxury
- Structure: Intro → Verses → Hooks → Outro
- Ad-libs: "UGH!", "MAG", "Yeah"

## Lyrical Style Notes

### DO:
- Use UK slang naturally throughout
- Reference UK locations and culture
- Keep boss/luxury themes from master
- Maintain deep, authoritative tone
- Include "UGH!" and "MAG" ad-libs

### DON'T:
- Use American slang (cap, fire, lit, etc.)
- Reference US locations
- Make it sound like drill (aggressive, fast)
- Lose the luxury/boss aesthetic
- Overuse slang (keep it natural)
