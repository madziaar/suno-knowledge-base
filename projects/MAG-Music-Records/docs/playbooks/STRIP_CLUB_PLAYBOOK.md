# Strip Club Playbook

Production rules for adult/club tracks optimized for that environment.

## Core Principles

1. **Bass is King** — Low-end drives everything
2. **Repetition Sells** — Hooks need to stick instantly
3. **Energy Management** — Build, drop, maintain
4. **Explicit is Expected** — Don't hold back (when appropriate)

## Audio Requirements

### Bass
- Sub frequencies: 40-80 Hz present and clean
- 808 patterns: Simple, hard-hitting
- Sidechain: Kick punches through bass
- "The bass should rattle speakers"

### BPM Range
| Style | BPM | Notes |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Slow grind | 65-75 | Sensual, tease |
| Standard club | 85-100 | Versatile |
| Drill/trap | 140-150 | High energy |

### Structure for Club Play
```
[Intro] 8 bars - Build anticipation
[Drop/Hook] 8-16 bars - Hit hard
[Verse] 16 bars - Maintain energy
[Hook] 8-16 bars - Repeat
[Verse 2] 16 bars - Build again
[Hook] 8-16 bars - Peak energy
[Outro] 8 bars - Let it ride out
```

## Lyrics Guidelines

### Hook Requirements
- **Length:** 2-4 lines maximum
- **Repetition:** Same hook 3-4x per track
- **Simplicity:** Easy to remember/chant
- **Call-and-response:** Works with crowd participation

### Hook Examples (Patterns)
```
[2-word repeat pattern]
"Get low, get low, get low, get low"

[Single phrase repeat]
"Make it bounce, make it bounce"

[Call-response structure]
"When I say [X], you say [Y]"
```

### Verse Content
- Keep verses moving, no slow sections
- Rhyme scheme: Simple, consistent
- Avoid complex metaphors
- Direct, visceral language

### Language Mix (Multilingual Tracks)
- Hook: Primary language (most accessible)
- Verses: Can mix languages
- Bridge: Either language works

## Production Checklist

### Pre-Generation
- [ ] Hook is 2-4 lines, highly repeatable
- [ ] Prompt includes bass emphasis
- [ ] BPM matches intended vibe
- [ ] Explicit content is intentional

### Post-Generation
- [ ] Bass hits hard on quality speakers
- [ ] Hook is memorable after one listen
- [ ] No dead spots in energy
- [ ] Would work in actual club setting

### Club Test (Mental)
Ask yourself:
1. Would this make people move?
2. Is the drop satisfying?
3. Can someone who doesn't speak the language vibe to it?
4. Does it maintain energy for 3+ minutes?

## Content Boundaries

### Acceptable
- Sexual themes (explicit)
- Party/club lifestyle
- Confidence/swagger
- Provocative language

### Avoid
- Violence for shock value
- Truly offensive content
- Anything that crosses into harmful territory
- Content that could get flagged/removed

## Track Archetypes

### The Grinder
- Slow BPM (65-75)
- Sensual, seductive
- Heavy bass, minimal drums
- For: Slower dances

### The Banger
- Standard BPM (85-100)
- High energy hook
- Driving beat
- For: Main floor peak hours

### The Drill Club Track
- High BPM (140-150)
- Aggressive energy
- UK/NYC drill influence
- For: High energy sets
